Age Manager guide

How to sign your youth in and out on a Sunday morning. No technical know-how needed. New to this? Practise on the demo first.

On Sunday mornings

The youth site only works on Sundays, from 8:00am to 11:00am. Ideally Youth will sign themselves in & out. But, if your help is required, these are the pages you will use. The Live count and History are also in the menu at the top of every page, for youth as well as age managers:

  • Bulk sign in / outSign a whole group in or out at once
  • Sign one youth in / outThis is the same page youth use
  • Live countShows how many are still here. Youth can see it too
  • HistoryWho came on a past Sunday. Youth can see it too

How bulk sign in works

Bulk sign in lets you sign in (or out) lots of youth in one go, instead of one at a time. It works best on your phone.

  1. Open Bulk sign in / out (above) any time from 8:00am on a Sunday. Before 8:00am it simply tells you when sign in starts.
  2. The page knows the time. Until 9:30am it is for signing in and the button at the bottom says Bulk Sign In. From 9:30am it is for signing out and the button says Bulk Sign Out.
  3. Tap your group, for example u14, to show its names. Tap All to show everyone.
  4. Tick the box next to each youth who is here (sign in) or who is leaving (sign out). A youth who is already signed in shows the time instead of a box, because they are already done. When signing out, only youth who signed in have a box.
  5. Tick everyone first. You can tick names in more than one group. Then press the big button at the bottom once.
  6. You will see Bulk Submission Completed at the top of the page. If it says something like (2 already recorded), those youth had already been signed in, so nobody is counted twice.
  7. If you see Bulk submission failed, check your phone has signal and press the button again. Anyone who was already done will not be counted twice.
  8. The button turns grey once it has worked. To do more names, refresh the page.

Not sure it worked? Open the Live count (how many are in) or History (who signed in or out).

Signing one youth in or out?

Use the normal sign-in page, the same page every youth sees. Start typing the name, tap it in the list, then press Sign In or Sign Out. You do not need the bulk page for this.

Practise any time on the demo

The youth site only works on Sunday mornings, so you cannot practise on it during the week. The demo works every day, at any time. It pretends it is sign in (or sign out) time, and it has its own demo names and records, so nothing you do on the demo changes the real youth records. Use it to learn the steps above, or to show someone else how it works.

  • Practise bulk sign inThe demo version of bulk sign in
  • Practise bulk sign outOnly demo names that are signed in have a box
  • Practise signing one inThe demo version of the youth page
  • Practise signing one outThe same, for sign out
  • Demo live countSee your practice sign ins add up
  • Demo historySee your practice records

To practise signing out, first practise signing the same names in, then open the practice sign out link. You can tell you are on the demo because the menu bar at the top says DEMO (on the real pages it says AM or YOUTH), and the tab shows an orange D.
