Demo for age managers

Practise bulk sign in and sign out, any day, at any time. Nothing you do here changes the real youth records.

What is the demo?

The real site only works on Sunday mornings, so you cannot practise on it during the week. The demo works every day. It pretends it is sign in (or sign out) time, and it has its own demo names and records. You can tell you are on the demo because the menu bar at the top says DEMO-A (the real pages say AM) and the tab shows an orange D.

To practise signing out, first practise signing some names in, then open the practice sign out link. The S-in and S-out links in the menu do the same.
