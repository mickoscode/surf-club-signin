Demo for youth

Practise signing in and out, any day, at any time. Nothing you do here changes the real youth records.

What is the demo?

The real site only works on Sunday mornings, so you cannot practise on it during the week. The demo works every day. It pretends it is sign in (or sign out) time, and it has its own demo names and records. You can tell you are on the demo because the menu bar at the top says DEMO-Y (the real pages say YOUTH) and the tab shows an orange D.

Practise

  • Practise signing inStart typing a name, tap it in the list, then press Sign In
  • Practise signing outThe same, for sign out
  • Demo live countSee your practice sign ins add up

To practise signing out, first practise signing the same name in, then open the practice sign out link. The in and out links in the menu do the same.
