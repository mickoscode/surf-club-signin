About

This is a simple sign-in-out app designed for Sorrento Surf Life Saving Club.

It enables Youth to quickly log their attendance at the Sunday Sessions.
The goal is to provide a simple electronic replacement of the traditional paper sign-in sheets.

How the app works

The sign-in-out forms can only be used on Sunday mornings, during standard Youth session times.
i.e.
Sign in is accessible from 8:00am to 9:30am
Sign out is accessible from 9:30am to 11:00am.

Youth simply enter their name and click the "Sign In/Out" button.
Only registered Youth will be able to sign in. Unrecognised names will be rejected.

Age Managers have an additional feature to do bulk sign-in-out. See the Age Manager guide for details.

FAQ

I am a registered member, but my name is not listed (or is incorrect)

Please use paper sign-in backup, and discuss with an Age Manager, to get your name corrected (they will need to escalate to Mick).

I would like some extra features added to the app - e.g. tracking proficiencies/awards, etc

This is not the purpose of the app... but if used successfully for sign-in-out, then perhaps this could be considered in future.

I am an Age Manager and I incorrectly signed someone in/out - how can I delete/correct this?

There is no way to change an attendance record after it has been created... please be more careful :)

The site currently says the next session is this weekend, but the season is over!

The sign-in-out application is not aware of the season dates... it simply assumes that sessions are every Sunday.
Please ignore the "The next session is ..." message when the season is over and during xmas break :)
