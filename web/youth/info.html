Youth Sign-in-out Guide

How to sign in and out at the Sunday sessions of Sorrento Surf Life Saving Club. It replaces the paper sign-in sheets, and takes a few seconds. New to this? Practise on the demo first.

On Sunday mornings

The sign in page only works on Sundays, from 8:00am to 11:00am. The menu at the top of every page (the red YOUTH brings you back here) has the same links:

  • Sign in / outLog your attendance
  • Live countShows how many are here right now
  • HistoryWho came on a past Sunday

How to sign in and out

  1. Open Sign in / out (above) on a Sunday morning. Before 8:00am it simply tells you when sign in starts.
  2. The page knows the time. Until 9:30am it is for signing in and the button says Sign In. From 9:30am it is for signing out and the button says Sign Out. Sign out closes at 11:00am.
  3. Start typing your name, then tap it in the list. Only registered youth can sign in, so a name that is not in the list is rejected.
  4. Press the button once. You will see your name with has signed in (or has signed out) at the top of the page.
  5. The button turns grey once it has worked. The page remembers your name for next time; press Clear Name if someone else needs to use your phone.

Not sure it worked? Open the Live count (how many are in) or History (who signed in or out).

Practise any time on the demo

The real page only works on Sunday mornings, so you cannot practise on it during the week. The demo works every day, at any time. It has its own demo names and records, so nothing you do on the demo changes the real records.

Questions

My name is not listed (or is incorrect)

Please use the paper sign-in backup, and talk to an Age Manager to get your name corrected (they will need to escalate to Mick).

The site says the next session is this weekend, but the season is over

The app does not know the season dates. It simply assumes there is a session every Sunday, so please ignore the "The next session is ..." message when the season is over and during the Christmas break.

Can you add extra features, like tracking proficiencies or awards?

That is not the purpose of the app, but if it works well for signing in and out, it could be considered in future.
